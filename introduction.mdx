CIPP (CyberDrain Improved Partner Portal) is an open-source Microsoft 365 management platform built for Managed Service Providers and Microsoft Partners. The standard Microsoft partner tooling makes multitenant administration slow and fragmented — CIPP replaces that with a single portal where you can manage users, enforce security baselines, handle email, configure devices, and monitor security across every client tenant you support. The goal is straightforward: remove the daily gripes of partner management and save several hours per engineer each month.

## Who CIPP is for

CIPP is designed for **Microsoft Partners** — primarily Managed Service Providers (MSPs) and IT service providers who manage Microsoft 365 environments on behalf of multiple customer organizations. If you hold a GDAP or legacy Delegated Admin relationship with your customers, CIPP gives you a unified control plane for those tenants.

CIPP also supports **direct tenant mode** for organizations that want to manage a single Microsoft 365 environment without a partner relationship.

## Open-source and self-hosted

CIPP is licensed under the [AGPL-3.0 license](https://github.com/KelvinTegelaar/CIPP/blob/main/LICENSE) and is free to self-host. You deploy it into your own Azure subscription, so your credentials and customer data never leave your environment.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy to Azure">
    Use the Azure Resource Manager template in the repository to provision the CIPP backend (Azure Functions) and frontend (Azure Static Web App) into your Azure tenant. See [Deploy CIPP to Azure](/setup/deployment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the setup wizard">
    Open the setup wizard to create the SAM application registration, authenticate with your partner tenant, and connect your first customer tenants. See [Onboard tenants with the CIPP setup wizard](/setup/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    Verify that your application registration has the correct Microsoft Graph permissions and that your GDAP relationships include the required Azure AD roles. See [CIPP roles and permissions](/setup/permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>
