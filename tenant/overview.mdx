CIPP gives you a unified control plane for every Microsoft 365 tenant in your partner portfolio. Rather than switching between customer portals, you can manage tenants, review security posture, inspect audit logs, handle subscriptions, and configure authentication policies — all without leaving a single interface. The tenant selector at the top of every page scopes your view instantly, so you can work across dozens or hundreds of customers without losing context.

## Multi-tenant management

The tenant selector appears in the top navigation bar throughout CIPP. Selecting a tenant scopes pages such as the domains list, audit logs, and authentication methods to that customer. Pages that support all-tenants mode — such as the tenants list itself — show data across your entire portfolio in a single table.

<Tip>
Use the tenants list at **Tenant Administration → Tenants** to open admin portals (Exchange, Entra, SharePoint, Teams, Azure, Intune) for any customer with one click. Quick-access portal links are surfaced directly in the table row.
</Tip>

## Backup and configuration history

CIPP can capture snapshots of tenant configuration so you can detect drift and restore settings when needed. Configuration backups are managed per-tenant from the tenant list's **Configure Backup** action.

<Info>
Backup and drift management capabilities are covered in detail under the Standards & Baselines section. See [Standards & Baselines](/tenant/standards-baselines) for how to detect and remediate configuration drift across your tenant portfolio.
</Info>
