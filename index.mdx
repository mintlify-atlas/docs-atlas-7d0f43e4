CIPP (CyberDrain Improved Partner Portal) is a Microsoft 365 multitenant management platform designed for Managed Service Providers and Microsoft Partners. From a single interface, you can manage users, tenants, email, devices, security, and compliance policies across all your client organizations — saving hours of manual work every week.

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy to Azure">
    Use the Azure deployment template to spin up the CIPP backend and frontend infrastructure in your Azure tenant. See [Deployment](/setup/deployment) for full instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure permissions and onboard">
    Run the setup wizard to create your application registration, connect to your tenants, and configure baselines and alerts. See [Onboarding](/setup/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start managing tenants">
    Browse to your CIPP URL, select a tenant from the tenant switcher, and begin administering users, email, devices, and security from one dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  CIPP requires a Microsoft Partner relationship (GDAP or legacy delegated admin) with the tenants you want to manage. Direct tenant mode is also supported for standalone tenants.
</Note>
